# Audio credits

Every file in `assets/audio/` is listed here with its source and licence. Nothing ships
without a licence line. All entries permit commercial use in a public repository.

Files marked **local library** come from Jurek's licensed universal sound library
(`~/Downloads/Claude/assets/universal audio/`, `~/Downloads/Claude/assets/adventure/audio/`).
The originals are 48 kHz / 44.1 kHz WAV; the repo carries trimmed, loudness-matched,
loop-clean MP3 renders only. Raw WAVs are never committed.

Files marked **CC0** were fetched from Freesound (Creative Commons Zero, public domain)
or Kenney (CC0). Attribution is not legally required for CC0 — it is kept here anyway.
